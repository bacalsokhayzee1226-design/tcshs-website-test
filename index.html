About TCSHS

A Quick Glance at TCSHS

Toledo City Science High School is a public science high school in Toledo City, Cebu, under the Division of Toledo City. Since 1996, it has helped young Filipinos grow into curious, capable and responsible scientists.

  • Focus on science, technology, engineering and mathematics
  • Research and innovation opportunities for students
  • Learning guided by DepEd core values
